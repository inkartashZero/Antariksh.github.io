Research at the intersection.

Continual & Federated Learning

Federated Learning · Continual Learning · Computer Vision

Reinforcement Learning

I am interested in learning and decision-making in changing environments, particularly long-horizon credit assignment, exploration, memory and adaptation.

Reinforcement Learning · Sequential Decision Making
03

Computational Neuroscience & NeuroAI

My work at CHINTA explores goal-directed spatial learning through computational models of behaviour. I am interested in how principles of biological learning can inform artificial systems, and vice versa.

Computational Neuroscience · NeuroAI · Learning & Memory
